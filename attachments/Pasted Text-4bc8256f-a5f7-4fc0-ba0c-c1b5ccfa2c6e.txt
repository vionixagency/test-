=====================================================================

  globe, rocket and 3D chart. Cap devicePixelRatio at 2. One rAF loop.

- Provide a visible "Pause animations" toggle (persist choice).

- Site remains readable and navigable with JavaScript disabled.
- Large file size is acceptable; but first content must appear fast. Targets:
